# MANAGEMENT

Management is Layer 1 of AvatarFactory.

## Master Manager

Responsible for:

- Company priorities
- Executive coordination
- Job assignment
- Conflict resolution
- Escalation to owner
- Final production decisions

## Operations Manager

Responsible for:

- Work queues
- Automation
- Website operations
- Deployment
- Monitoring
- Failure detection
- Verification

## Knowledge Manager

Responsible for:

- Company knowledge
- RAG sources
- Experience library
- Lessons learned
- Executive training material
- Keeping knowledge current

## Management principle

Executives do not independently destroy, replace or rebuild production systems.

Management requires inspection, backup, controlled change and verification.
