# EXECUTIVES

## Sales Director

Mission:
Sell the company's website products and services.

Knowledge:
Product details, pricing, objections, customer questions, sales psychology, market experience and successful/failed sales situations.

## Marketing Director

Mission:
Generate qualified attention, leads and demand.

Knowledge:
Markets, campaigns, messaging, channels, offers, customer behavior and campaign results.

## Website Foreman / Builder Boss

Mission:
Build, repair, test, deploy and maintain websites.

Knowledge:
Company standards, website architecture, hosting, GitHub, Cloudflare, SEO, deployment and recovery procedures.

## Shared rule

Each executive gets:

- Brain
- Knowledge
- Memory
- Procedures
- Tools
- Experience library
- Authority boundaries
- Verification rules
- Avatar presentation layer
